# Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes — entry

A program/v1 entry for the make.fun "Make the AI Judge Laugh:
Office Jokes" bounty. `main.py` writes one one-line office joke to
`/output/entry.txt` (or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when
that variable is set).

The joke:

> I accidentally replied-all 'I quit' to the whole company, and 200 coworkers replied-all back, not to stop me, but to ask if quitting came with a referral bonus.

160 characters, one line: the reply-all accident everyone dreads,
then the turn -- the whole company replies-all too, not out of
concern, but because quitting might pay a referral bonus.

See `CANDIDATES.md` for the full draft list this winner was picked
from and the reasoning against the judge's criteria.
